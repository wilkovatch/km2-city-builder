# Moving around

These are the default controls. **Help → Controls** always shows the ones you are using, and they can be changed in the **Controls** tab of the preferences.

## The camera

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Pan | Drag with the middle mouse button |
| Rotate | Drag with the right mouse button |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel |
| Zoom in on a point | Click the middle mouse button without dragging |
| Show the selected object | F |

If your mouse has no middle button, turn on **Emulate 3-button mouse** in the **Controls** tab of the preferences. Alt + left button then acts as the middle button.

## Selecting

- Click an object to select it. Its properties open in the panel on the right, which usually has several tabs.
- Press Escape, or click **End editing**, to deselect it.
- Press Delete to delete the selected object or point.
- If a mesh is in the way, hold Ctrl and click through it.
- **Edit → View object list** lists every object in the city, which helps when one is hard to click.

Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y redoes almost anything you do to the city.

## Placing points

Roads, terrain patches and building lines are made of points. Their panels have a **Point placement mode** dropdown that says what a click on the ground does:

- With **None**, clicks only select existing points, which you can then drag to move.
- With the other modes, a click adds a point. **Add after last** is the usual one for roads.

A new object starts ready to add points. When you select an existing one, the mode is **None**, so a stray click doesn't change it.

## Moving things precisely

- Select a point and drag the gizmo to move it, or press M to type its position.
- Hold Ctrl while clicking to select several points.
- Press G to turn on **Snap to grid**. While it is on, a badge shows at the bottom left of the window.
- To place a point above the ground, hold Shift and move the mouse up or down. Tap Shift to go back to the ground.
