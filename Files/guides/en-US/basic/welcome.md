# Welcome

km2 City Builder is an editor for creating cities for videogames. You place roads, intersections, terrain and buildings, then export the city in the format of the game.

It is a tool, not a game: nothing moves in the editor, and you try the city by playing it in the game.

## Cores

The editor knows nothing about any game by itself. Each game is supported by a **core**, a small add-on that defines which elements exist, how they look and how they are exported.

This guide covers what is the same for every core. Once a core is installed, look in **Help → Guides** for its own guides, with the details of that game.

## How a city is made

1. Install a core (you only need to do this once).
2. Create a city.
3. Place the roads. Intersections appear where roads connect.
4. Fill the space between the roads with terrain.
5. Add the buildings.
6. Save, then export the city for the game.

> Try to finish the road network before adding terrain. Changing how the roads connect later can detach the terrain from them, and you may have to recreate those patches.

## Using this guide

You can keep this panel open while you work:

- Drag its title bar to move it, or its edges and corners to resize it.
- The button with a line in the title bar shrinks it to just the title, so you can see the city behind it.
- The chapters are listed on the left, and **Previous** and **Next** are under the text.
